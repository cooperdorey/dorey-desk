FactEpisode 7 · Relative frequency
A trial is one go of an experiment, such as one toss of a coin.
The relative frequency of an event is how often it happened, out of the number of trials:
$\text{relative frequency} = \dfrac{\text{number of times the event happens}}{\text{total number of trials}}$
Relative frequency is an estimate of the probability.
The more trials, the closer the relative frequency usually gets to the theoretical probability.

We Do 6Episode 7 · Relative frequency
A die is rolled $200$ times. It shows a six $50$ times.
(a) Find the relative frequency of rolling a six. 1
$\dfrac{50}{200} = 0.25$
(b) Is the die likely to be fair? Justify your answer. 2
For a fair die, $P(6) = \dfrac{1}{6} \approx 0.17$. With $200$ trials, $0.25$ is well above this, so the die is likely not fair (biased towards six).
