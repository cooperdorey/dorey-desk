FactEpisode 4 · Complementary events
The complement of an event is the event not happening.
An event and its complement cover every outcome, so their probabilities add to $1$.
$P(\text{an event does not occur}) = 1 - P(\text{the event does occur})$
$P(\text{rain}) = 0.3$, so $P(\text{no rain}) = 1 - 0.3 = 0.7$   (or $100\% - 30\% = 70\%$)

I DoEpisode 4 · Complementary events
The probability that it rains tomorrow is $0.35$.
Find the probability that it does not rain tomorrow.
$P(\text{no rain}) = 1 - 0.35 = 0.65$

We Do 4Episode 4 · Complementary events
A standard die is rolled.
Find $P(\text{not a } 6)$.
$P(6) = \dfrac{1}{6}$
$P(\text{not a } 6) = 1 - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{5}{6}$
We Do 5Episode 4 · Complementary events
A bag holds $4$ red, $5$ blue and $3$ green marbles. One marble is taken out at random.
Find the probability that it is not green.
Total $= 12$,   $P(\text{green}) = \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{1}{4}$
$P(\text{not green}) = 1 - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{3}{4}$
We Do 6Episode 4 · Complementary events
Tomorrow will be sunny, cloudy or rainy.
$P(\text{sunny}) = 0.45$ and $P(\text{cloudy}) = 0.4$.
Find $P(\text{rainy})$.
The probabilities add to $1$
$P(\text{rainy}) = 1 - (0.45 + 0.4) = 1 - 0.85 = 0.15$
We Do 7Episode 4 · Complementary events
A wheel has the numbers $1$ to $25$ marked on it. When the wheel is spun, each number is equally likely to win.
(a) List all the multiples of $3$ on the wheel that are greater than $10$. 1
$12,\ 15,\ 18,\ 21,\ 24$
(b) What is the probability that the winning number is NOT a multiple of $3$ greater than $10$? 2
$P(\text{multiple of 3 greater than 10}) = \dfrac{5}{25} = \dfrac{1}{5}$
$P(\text{NOT}) = 1 - \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{4}{5}$
