FactEpisode 9 · Comparing rates and best buys
To compare rates, write each one as a unit rate in the same units.
The best buy is the item with the lowest unit price.
$\$6.60$ for 3 kg $= \$2.20$/kg   vs   $\$10.50$ for 5 kg $= \$2.10$/kg
Routine · modelEpisode 9 · Comparing rates and best buys
A 500 g block of cheese costs $\$4.00$.
Find the price per 100 g.
500 g $= 5 \times 100$ g
$\$4.00 \div 5 = \$0.80$ per 100 g

I DoEpisode 9 · Comparing rates and best buys
Which is the better buy:
2 kg of rice for $\$5.00$, or 5 kg of rice for $\$11.50$?
2 kg: $5.00 \div 2 = \$2.50$/kg
5 kg: $11.50 \div 5 = \$2.30$/kg
The 5 kg bag is the better buy

We Do 6Episode 9 · Comparing rates and best buys
