FactEpisode 7 · Rates and unit rates
A rate compares two quantities measured in different units, using "per" or "/".
A unit rate is the amount for 1 of the second quantity.
180 km in 2 hours  →  $180 \div 2 = 90$ km/h

I DoEpisode 7 · Rates and unit rates
A tap fills a bucket with 30 L of water in 5 minutes.
Write this as a unit rate in L/min.
$30 \div 5 = 6$
Unit rate $= 6$ L/min
We Do 1Episode 7 · Rates and unit rates
A car travels 180 km in 2 hours.
Write this as a unit rate in km/h.
$180 \div 2 = 90$
Unit rate $= 90$ km/h
We Do 2Episode 7 · Rates and unit rates
Tom earns $\$162$ for 6 hours of work.
Write his pay as a unit rate in dollars per hour.
$162 \div 6 = 27$
Unit rate $= \$27$/h
We Do 3Episode 7 · Rates and unit rates
A 3 kg bag of apples costs $\$8.70$.
Find the cost per kilogram.
$8.70 \div 3 = 2.90$
Unit rate $= \$2.90$/kg
We Do 4Episode 7 · Rates and unit rates
A heart beats 35 times in 30 seconds.
Write this as a rate in beats per minute.
1 minute $= 60$ s $= 2 \times 30$ s
$35 \times 2 = 70$
Rate $= 70$ beats/min
