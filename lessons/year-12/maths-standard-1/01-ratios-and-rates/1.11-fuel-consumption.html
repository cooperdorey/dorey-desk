FactEpisode 11 · Fuel consumption
Fuel consumption is the number of litres a vehicle uses to travel 100 km, in L/100 km.
$\text{Fuel consumption} = \dfrac{\text{litres used}}{\text{km travelled}} \times 100$
A lower fuel consumption means a more efficient vehicle.
36 L for 400 km:  $\dfrac{36}{400} \times 100 = 9$ L/100 km

We Do 4Episode 11 · Fuel consumption
A car uses $7.5$ L/100 km. Petrol costs $\$1.95$ per litre.
Find the cost of fuel for a 420 km trip.
Fuel $= 7.5 \times 4.2 = 31.5$ L
Cost $= 31.5 \times \$1.95 = \$61.43$
We Do 5Episode 11 · Fuel consumption
A car uses 9 L/100 km. Its tank holds 54 L.
How far can it travel on a full tank?
$54 \div 9 = 6$ lots of 100 km
Distance $= 6 \times 100 = 600$ km
We Do 6Episode 11 · Fuel consumption
Car A uses 45 L to travel 600 km. Car B uses 52 L to travel 650 km.
Which car is more efficient?
Car A: $\dfrac{45}{600} \times 100 = 7.5$ L/100 km
Car B: $\dfrac{52}{650} \times 100 = 8$ L/100 km
Car A uses less fuel per 100 km, so it is more efficient
We Do 7Episode 11 · Fuel consumption
A driver travels 15 000 km a year. Petrol costs $\$2.00$ per litre.
Car A uses 6 L/100 km and Car B uses 9 L/100 km. How much does Car A save each year?
15 000 km $= 150$ lots of 100 km
Car A: $6 \times 150 = 900$ L $\to \$1800$
Car B: $9 \times 150 = 1350$ L $\to \$2700$
Car A saves $\$2700 - \$1800 = \$900$ a year
