FactEpisode 13 · Scale drawings with a ruler
A scale of $1 : n$ means 1 cm on the drawing is $n$ cm in real life.
$\text{Real length} = \text{drawing length} \times n$
A scale bar or a grid square can also give the scale: it shows what one length on the drawing stands for.
Scale $1 : 50$, drawing $3$ cm:  $3 \times 50 = 150$ cm $= 1.5$ m

We Do 6Episode 13 · Scale drawings with a ruler
A map has scale $1 : 25\,000$. The distance between two huts on the map is 6 cm.
A hiker walks between the huts at 3 km/h. How long does it take?
$6 \times 25\,000 = 150\,000$ cm $= 1.5$ km
Time $= 1.5 \div 3 = 0.5$ h
It takes 30 minutes
