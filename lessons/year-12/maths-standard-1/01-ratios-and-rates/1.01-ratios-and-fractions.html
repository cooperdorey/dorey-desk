FactEpisode 1 · Ratios and fractions
A ratio compares two or more quantities.
It is written $a : b$ and read "$a$ to $b$".
3 apples and 5 oranges: the ratio of apples to oranges is $3 : 5$.

We Do 6Episode 1 · Ratios and fractions
In a class, the ratio of boys to girls is $4 : 7$.
The number of boys is what fraction of the number of girls?
Boys $=$ 4 parts, girls $=$ 7 parts, so $\dfrac{4}{7}$
We Do 7Episode 1 · Ratios and fractions
$\frac{2}{5}$ of a class walk to school. The rest do not.
Write the ratio of walkers to non-walkers.
Non-walkers $= 1 - \dfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{3}{5}$, so walkers to non-walkers $= 2 : 3$
