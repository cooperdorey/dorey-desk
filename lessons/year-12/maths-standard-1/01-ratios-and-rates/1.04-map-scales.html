FactEpisode 4 · Map scales
A map scale of $1 : n$ means 1 unit on the map represents $n$ of the same units in real life.
$1 : 1000$  means  1 cm on the map $= 1000$ cm $= 10$ m in real life.
Routine · modelEpisode 4 · Map scales
Convert $250\,000$ cm to km.
$250\,000 \div 100 = 2500$ m
$2500 \div 1000 = 2.5$ km

We Do 3Episode 4 · Map scales
A map has a scale of $1 : 50\,000$.
A distance on the map is $3.5$ cm. What is the actual distance in km?
Actual $= 3.5 \times 50\,000 = 175\,000$ cm
$175\,000 \div 100 = 1750$ m
$1750 \div 1000 = 1.75$ km
We Do 4Episode 4 · Map scales
A plan has a scale of $1 : 200$.
A wall is 12 m long. How long is the wall on the plan?
$12$ m $= 1200$ cm
Plan $= 1200 \div 200 = 6$ cm
