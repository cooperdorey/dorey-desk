FactEpisode 10 · Speed, distance and time
$\text{Speed} = \dfrac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$     $\text{Distance} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$     $\text{Time} = \dfrac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$
On a distance–time graph, a steeper line means a faster speed, and a flat line means stopped.

I DoEpisode 10 · Speed, distance and time
A bus travels at 60 km/h for 3 hours.
How far does it travel?
$D = S \times T$
$D = 60 \times 3 = 180$ km
We Do 1Episode 10 · Speed, distance and time
A car travels at 80 km/h for 2 hours.
How far does it travel?
$D = S \times T$
$D = 80 \times 2 = 160$ km
We Do 2Episode 10 · Speed, distance and time
A truck travels 240 km at 60 km/h.
How long does the trip take?
$T = \dfrac{D}{S}$
$T = 240 \div 60 = 4$ h
We Do 3Episode 10 · Speed, distance and time
A cyclist rides 150 km in 2 hours 30 minutes.
Find the average speed.
2 h 30 min $= 2.5$ h
$S = \dfrac{D}{T} = 150 \div 2.5 = 60$ km/h
We Do 4Episode 10 · Speed, distance and time
A train travels 210 km at 90 km/h.
How long does the trip take, in hours and minutes?
$T = 210 \div 90 = 2.333\ldots$ h
$0.333\ldots \times 60 = 20$ min
$T = 2$ h $20$ min

We Do 6Episode 10 · Speed, distance and time
