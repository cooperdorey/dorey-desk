Building plans use symbols:

I DoEpisode 5 · Scale drawings and building plans
The plan of a study has a scale of $1 : 100$.
Find the real length and width of the study in metres.
Length $= 3 \times 100 = 300$ cm $= 3$ m
Width $= 2.5 \times 100 = 250$ cm $= 2.5$ m

Find the real length and width of the bedroom in metres.
Length $= 4 \times 100 = 400$ cm $= 4$ m
Width $= 3 \times 100 = 300$ cm $= 3$ m
We Do 2Episode 5 · Scale drawings and building plans
Find the real floor area of the bedroom.
Real size $= 4$ m $\times 3$ m
Area $= 4 \times 3 = 12$ m$^2$
We Do 3Episode 5 · Scale drawings and building plans
Carpet costs $\$45$ per m$^2$.
Find the cost to carpet the bedroom.
Area $= 12$ m$^2$
Cost $= 12 \times \$45 = \$540$

We Do 5Episode 5 · Scale drawings and building plans
The living room and kitchen are tiled.
Tiles cost $\$32$ per m$^2$. Find the total cost.
Living $= 5 \times 4 = 20$ m$^2$,   Kitchen $= 3 \times 4 = 12$ m$^2$
Total area $= 20 + 12 = 32$ m$^2$
Cost $= 32 \times \$32 = \$1024$
We Do 6Episode 5 · Scale drawings and building plans
The bathroom floor is tiled with square tiles 50 cm by 50 cm.
How many tiles are needed?
Bathroom $= 2$ m $\times 1.5$ m $= 3$ m$^2$
One tile $= 0.5 \times 0.5 = 0.25$ m$^2$
Tiles $= 3 \div 0.25 = 12$ tiles

For the living room, which is cheaper:
carpet at $\$45$ per m$^2$, or a floating floor at $\$38$ per m$^2$ plus $\$150$ delivery?
Area $= 20$ m$^2$
Carpet $= 20 \times \$45 = \$900$
Floating floor $= 20 \times \$38 + \$150 = \$910$
Carpet is cheaper by $\$10$
