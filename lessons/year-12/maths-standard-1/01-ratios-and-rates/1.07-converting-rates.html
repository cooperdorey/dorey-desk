FactEpisode 7 · Converting rates
To convert a rate, change one unit at a time.
Write the rate as a fraction, then replace each unit with its value in the new unit.
$90$ km/h $= \dfrac{90\,000 \text{ m}}{3600 \text{ s}} = 25$ m/s

We Do 3Episode 7 · Converting rates
Convert 90 km/h to m/s.
90 km $= 90\,000$ m,   1 h $= 3600$ s
$90\,000 \div 3600 = 25$
$90$ km/h $= 25$ m/s
We Do 4Episode 7 · Converting rates
Convert 15 m/s to km/h.
In 1 h: $15 \times 3600 = 54\,000$ m
$54\,000$ m $= 54$ km
$15$ m/s $= 54$ km/h
We Do 5Episode 7 · Converting rates
A tap drips 500 mL every minute.
Convert this rate to L/h.
In 1 h: $500 \times 60 = 30\,000$ mL
$30\,000$ mL $= 30$ L
$500$ mL/min $= 30$ L/h
We Do 6Episode 7 · Converting rates
A drip feeds $4.5$ L of fluid each hour.
Convert this rate to mL/min.
$4.5$ L $= 4500$ mL
$4500 \div 60 = 75$
$4.5$ L/h $= 75$ mL/min
