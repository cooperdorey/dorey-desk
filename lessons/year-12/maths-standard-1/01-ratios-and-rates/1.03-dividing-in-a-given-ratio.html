FactEpisode 3 · Dividing in a given ratio
To divide a quantity in a ratio:
1. Find the total number of parts.
2. Find the value of one part.
3. Multiply each number in the ratio by one part.
$\$20$ in the ratio $2 : 3$:  $5$ parts, so one part $= \$4$.

$\$35$ is shared in the ratio $3 : 4$.
What is one part worth?
Total parts $= 3 + 4 = 7$
One part $= \$35 \div 7 = \$5$

I DoEpisode 3 · Dividing in a given ratio
Share $\$30$ between Jo and Sam in the ratio $1 : 4$.
Total parts $= 1 + 4 = 5$
One part $= \$30 \div 5 = \$6$
Jo $= 1 \times \$6 = \$6$,   Sam $= 4 \times \$6 = \$24$
Check: $\$6 + \$24 = \$30$

We Do 4Episode 3 · Dividing in a given ratio
The ratio of boys to girls in a class is $3 : 4$.
There are 12 boys. How many girls are there?
Boys are $3$ parts $= 12$
One part $= 12 \div 3 = 4$
Girls $= 4 \times 4 = 16$
We Do 5Episode 3 · Dividing in a given ratio
A drink is made from cordial and water in the ratio $1 : 5$.
How much water is needed to make $1.2$ L of drink?
Total parts $= 1 + 5 = 6$
One part $= 1.2 \div 6 = 0.2$ L
Water $= 5 \times 0.2 = 1$ L $(1000$ mL$)$
We Do 6Episode 3 · Dividing in a given ratio
Kai and Lena share a $\$4500$ profit in the ratio $2 : 3$.
How much more does Lena get than Kai?
Total parts $= 2 + 3 = 5$
One part $= \$4500 \div 5 = \$900$
Kai $= \$1800$,   Lena $= \$2700$
Lena gets $\$2700 - \$1800 = \$900$ more
We Do 7Episode 3 · Dividing in a given ratio
Two shares are in the ratio $3 : 5$.
The difference between the shares is $\$40$. What is the total amount?
Difference $= 5 - 3 = 2$ parts $= \$40$
One part $= \$40 \div 2 = \$20$
Total parts $= 3 + 5 = 8$
Total $= 8 \times \$20 = \$160$
