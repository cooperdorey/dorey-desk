I DoEpisode 2 · Pythagoras in practice
A $4$ m ladder leans against a wall. Its foot is $1$ m from the wall.
How high up the wall does it reach, correct to $2$ decimal places?
$h^2 = 4^2 - 1^2 = 15$
$h = \sqrt{15} = 3.872\ldots \approx 3.87$ m

We Do 2Episode 2 · Pythagoras in practice
A rectangular TV screen is $1.2$ m wide and $0.7$ m high.
Find the length of its diagonal, correct to $2$ decimal places.
$d^2 = 1.2^2 + 0.7^2 = 1.93$
$d = \sqrt{1.93} = 1.389\ldots \approx 1.39$ m
We Do 3Episode 2 · Pythagoras in practice
Kai walks $300$ m due east, then $400$ m due north.
How far is he from his starting point, in a straight line?
$d^2 = 300^2 + 400^2 = 250\,000$
$d = 500$ m
We Do 4Episode 2 · Pythagoras in practice
A rectangular park is $80$ m long and $60$ m wide.
How much shorter is it to walk diagonally across the park than to walk along two sides?
Two sides: $80 + 60 = 140$ m
Diagonal: $\sqrt{80^2 + 60^2} = \sqrt{10\,000} = 100$ m
Saving: $140 - 100 = 40$ m
We Do 5Episode 2 · Pythagoras in practice
A ship sails $162$ m due north, then $45$ m due west.
How far is the ship from its starting point, correct to the nearest metre?
Draw the triangle: the two legs are $162$ m and $45$ m
$d^2 = 162^2 + 45^2 = 28\,269$
$d = 168.13\ldots \approx 168$ m
We Do 6Episode 2 · Pythagoras in practice
A plan of a triangular paddock $ABC$ is drawn with a scale of $1 : 500$. The angle at $B$ is a right angle.
(a) On the plan, $AB$ measures $12.8$ cm. Find the actual length of $AB$ in metres. 2
$12.8 \times 500 = 6400$ cm $= 64$ m
(b) The length of $AB$ is equal to the length of $BC$. Find the length of $AC$, correct to $3$ significant figures. 3
$AC^2 = 64^2 + 64^2 = 8192$
$AC = 90.50\ldots \approx 90.5$ m
