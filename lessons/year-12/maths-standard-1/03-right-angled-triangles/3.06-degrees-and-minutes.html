FactEpisode 6 · Degrees and minutes
Angles can be measured in degrees and minutes, like hours and minutes:
$1^\circ = 60'$
$35^\circ30'$ means $35$ degrees and $30$ minutes, which is $35.5^\circ$.
On the calculator, the ° ' " (DMS) key enters and converts degrees and minutes.

We Do 5Episode 6 · Degrees and minutes
A tree casts a shadow $14$ m long. The sun's rays make an angle of $38^\circ24'$ with the ground.
Find the height of the tree, correct to $2$ decimal places.
$\tan 38^\circ24' = \dfrac{h}{14}$
$h = 14\tan 38^\circ24' = 11.096\ldots \approx 11.10$ m
