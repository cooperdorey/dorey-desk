FactEpisode 1 · Pythagoras' theorem
The hypotenuse is the longest side of a right-angled triangle. It is opposite the right angle.
Pythagoras' theorem: if $c$ is the hypotenuse and $a$ and $b$ are the shorter sides,
$c^2 = a^2 + b^2$
Hypotenuse: add the squares.   Shorter side: subtract the squares.
