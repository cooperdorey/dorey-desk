FactEpisode 3 · The trigonometric ratios
Name the sides from the marked angle $\theta$:
• Hypotenuse (H): opposite the right angle (longest side)
• Opposite (O): across from $\theta$
• Adjacent (A): next to $\theta$, but not the hypotenuse
If the angle moves, O and A swap. H never changes.

FactEpisode 3 · The trigonometric ratios
The trigonometric ratios for an angle $\theta$ in a right-angled triangle:
$\sin\theta = \dfrac{O}{H} \qquad \cos\theta = \dfrac{A}{H} \qquad \tan\theta = \dfrac{O}{A}$
SOH   CAH   TOA
