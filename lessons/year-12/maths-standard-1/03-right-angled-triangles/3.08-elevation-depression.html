FactEpisode 8 · Angles of elevation and depression
Angles are measured from the horizontal to the line of sight.
• Angle of elevation: looking up from the horizontal
• Angle of depression: looking down from the horizontal (marked $\theta$ below)
The angle of depression from A to B equals the angle of elevation from B to A (alternate angles).

I DoEpisode 8 · Angles of elevation and depression
From a point $30$ m from the base of a tree, the angle of elevation to the top of the tree is $40^\circ$.
Find the height of the tree, correct to $2$ decimal places.
$\tan 40^\circ = \dfrac{h}{30}$
$h = 30\tan 40^\circ = 25.17$ m

We Do 2Episode 8 · Angles of elevation and depression
From the top of a $60$ m cliff, the angle of depression to a boat is $25^\circ$.
How far is the boat from the base of the cliff, correct to $2$ decimal places?
The angle at the boat is also $25^\circ$ (alternate angles)
$\tan 25^\circ = \dfrac{60}{x}$
$x = \dfrac{60}{\tan 25^\circ} = 128.67$ m
We Do 3Episode 8 · Angles of elevation and depression
A building is $25$ m tall. From a point $40$ m from its base, find the angle of elevation to the top, correct to the nearest degree.
$\tan\theta = \dfrac{25}{40}$
$\theta = 32.0\ldots^\circ \approx 32^\circ$
We Do 4Episode 8 · Angles of elevation and depression
A lookout is $120$ m above sea level. A boat is $450$ m from the base of the lookout.
Find the angle of depression from the lookout to the boat, correct to the nearest minute.
$\tan\theta = \dfrac{120}{450}$
$\theta = 14.931\ldots^\circ = 14^\circ56'$
We Do 5Episode 8 · Angles of elevation and depression
From the top of a $45$ m tower, the angle of depression to a car is $18^\circ$.
Find the distance along the line of sight from the top of the tower to the car, correct to $2$ decimal places.
$\sin 18^\circ = \dfrac{45}{d}$
$d = \dfrac{45}{\sin 18^\circ} = 145.62$ m
We Do 6Episode 8 · Angles of elevation and depression
From the top of a lighthouse $40$ m above sea level, the angle of depression to a boat is $12^\circ$.
(a) How far is the boat from the base of the lighthouse, to $2$ decimal places? 2
$x = \dfrac{40}{\tan 12^\circ} = 188.19$ m
(b) The boat sails $50$ m directly towards the lighthouse. Find the new angle of depression, to the nearest degree. 2
New distance $= 188.19 - 50 = 138.19$ m
$\tan\theta = \dfrac{40}{138.19}$,   $\theta = 16.14\ldots^\circ \approx 16^\circ$
