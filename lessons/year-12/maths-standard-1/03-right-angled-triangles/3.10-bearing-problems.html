FactEpisode 10 · Bearing problems
Solving a bearing problem
1. Draw the diagram with a north line at the starting point.
2. Mark the right angle and the known lengths.
3. Use Pythagoras for a distance and $\tan^{-1}$ (or $\sin^{-1}$, $\cos^{-1}$) for an angle.
4. Turn the angle into a bearing, measured from north at the right point.
“The bearing of $B$ from $A$” is measured at $A$.
I DoEpisode 10 · Bearing problems
Kai walks $3$ km due north, then $4$ km due east.
(a) How far is he from his start?
(b) Find his true bearing from his start, to the nearest degree.
(a) $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$ km
(b) $\tan\theta = \dfrac{4}{3}$,   $\theta = 53.13\ldots^\circ$
Measured clockwise from north: $053^\circ$T

We Do 2Episode 10 · Bearing problems
A boat sails $5$ km due south, then $2$ km due east.
Find its true bearing from its start, to the nearest degree.
$\tan\theta = \dfrac{2}{5}$,   $\theta = 21.80^\circ \approx 22^\circ$ (from south)
True bearing: $180^\circ - 22^\circ = 158^\circ$T
We Do 3Episode 10 · Bearing problems
A plane flies $7$ km due south, then $4$ km due west.
Find its true bearing from its start, to the nearest degree.
$\tan\theta = \dfrac{4}{7}$,   $\theta = 29.74^\circ \approx 30^\circ$ (from south, towards west)
True bearing: $180^\circ + 30^\circ = 210^\circ$T
We Do 4Episode 10 · Bearing problems
A ship sails $20$ km on a bearing of $050^\circ$T.
(a) How far east of its start is it?
(b) How far north of its start is it? Give both to $2$ decimal places.
The $20$ km path is the hypotenuse, $50^\circ$ from north
(a) East: $20\sin 50^\circ = 15.32$ km
(b) North: $20\cos 50^\circ = 12.86$ km
We Do 5Episode 10 · Bearing problems
A hiker walks $240$ m due north, then $60$ m due west.
(a) How far is the hiker from the start, to the nearest metre?
(b) Find the true bearing of the hiker from the start, to the nearest degree.
(a) $\sqrt{240^2 + 60^2} = 247.39 \approx 247$ m
(b) $\tan\theta = \dfrac{60}{240}$,   $\theta = 14.04^\circ \approx 14^\circ$ west of north
True bearing: $360^\circ - 14^\circ = 346^\circ$T
We Do 6Episode 10 · Bearing problems
$B$ is $75$ m due east of $A$. $C$ is $75$ m due north of $B$.
(a) Find the length of $AC$, correct to $3$ significant figures. 2
$\sqrt{75^2 + 75^2} = 106.06\ldots \approx 106$ m
(b) Find the true bearing of $A$ from $C$. 2
At $C$: $\tan\theta = \dfrac{75}{75} = 1$, so $\theta = 45^\circ$ west of south
True bearing: $180^\circ + 45^\circ = 225^\circ$T
