FactEpisode 9 · True and compass bearings
A true bearing is measured clockwise from north, written with three digits and T.
Examples: $060^\circ$T,   $150^\circ$T,   $315^\circ$T
FactEpisode 9 · True and compass bearings
A compass bearing starts at N or S, then turns towards E or W.
Examples: N$60^\circ$E,   S$30^\circ$E,   N$45^\circ$W
The angle in a compass bearing is always between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$.
