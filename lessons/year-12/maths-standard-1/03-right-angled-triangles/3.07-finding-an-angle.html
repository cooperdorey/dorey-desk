FactEpisode 7 · Finding an angle
To find an angle, use the inverse ratio (SHIFT then sin, cos or tan on the calculator):
$\sin\theta = 0.5 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \theta = \sin^{-1}(0.5) = 30^\circ$
The same works for $\cos^{-1}$ and $\tan^{-1}$.
Name the sides, choose the ratio, write it as a fraction, then use the inverse.
