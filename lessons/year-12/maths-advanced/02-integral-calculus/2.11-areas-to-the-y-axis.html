I DoEpisode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
Find the area between $y=x^{2}$ (for $x\geq0$), the $y$-axis and the line $y=9$.
Make $x$ the subject: $x=\sqrt{y}=y^{\frac{1}{2}}$.
The limits are $y=0$ (the vertex) to $y=9$.
$\displaystyle A=\int_{0}^{9}y^{\frac{1}{2}}\,dy=\left[\dfrac{2}{3}y^{\frac{3}{2}}\right]_{0}^{9}=\dfrac{2}{3}\times27=18$ square units
We Do 1Episode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
Find the area between $y=x^{2}+3$ (for $x\geq0$), the $y$-axis and the lines $y=4$ and $y=7$.
$x=\sqrt{y-3}=(y-3)^{\frac{1}{2}}$
$\displaystyle A=\int_{4}^{7}(y-3)^{\frac{1}{2}}\,dy=\left[\dfrac{2}{3}(y-3)^{\frac{3}{2}}\right]_{4}^{7}=\dfrac{2}{3}\left(8-1\right)=\dfrac{14}{3}$ square units
FactEpisode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
$y=\log_{a}x$ is the reflection of $y=a^{x}$ in $y=x$
Area between $y=\log_{a}x$ and the $y$-axis for $c\leq y\leq d$
$=$ area between $y=a^{x}$ and the $x$-axis for $c\leq x\leq d$
I DoEpisode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
Find the area between $y=\ln x$, the $y$-axis and the lines $y=0$ and $y=2$.
Make $x$ the subject: $x=e^{y}$.
$\displaystyle A=\int_{0}^{2}e^{y}\,dy=[e^{y}]_{0}^{2}=e^{2}-1$ square units
This is also the area under $y=e^{x}$ from $x=0$ to $x=2$, the reflection of the region.
We Do 2Episode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
Find the exact area between $y=\log_{2}x$, the $y$-axis and the lines $y=1$ and $y=3$.
$x=2^{y}$
$\displaystyle A=\int_{1}^{3}2^{y}\,dy=\left[\dfrac{2^{y}}{\ln2}\right]_{1}^{3}=\dfrac{8-2}{\ln2}=\dfrac{6}{\ln2}$ square units
We Do 3Episode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
Find the exact area between $y=\dfrac{2}{x}$, the $y$-axis and the lines $y=1$ and $y=4$.
$x=\dfrac{2}{y}$
$\displaystyle A=\int_{1}^{4}\dfrac{2}{y}\,dy=[2\ln y]_{1}^{4}=2\ln4=4\ln2$ square units
We Do 4Episode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
Find the area between $y=x^{3}$, the $y$-axis and the lines $y=-8$ and $y=8$.
$x=y^{\frac{1}{3}}$, which is negative for $y\lt 0$.
$\int_{0}^{8}y^{\frac{1}{3}}\,dy=\left[\dfrac{3}{4}y^{\frac{4}{3}}\right]_{0}^{8}=\dfrac{3}{4}\times16=12$
$\int_{-8}^{0}y^{\frac{1}{3}}\,dy=-12$, so this area is $12$
$A=12+12=24$ square units
We Do 5Episode 11 · Areas to the $y$-axis
The region is bounded by $y=e^{x}$, the $y$-axis and the line $y=e$. Find its exact area.
The curve meets $y=e$ at $x=1$.
The rectangle $0\leq x\leq1$, $0\leq y\leq e$ has area $e$.
Area under the curve: $\int_{0}^{1}e^{x}\,dx=e-1$
$A=e-(e-1)=1$ square unit
