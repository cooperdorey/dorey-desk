Category · ruleEpisode 9 · Mixed integrals
Which integral? Look at the form.
$x^{n}$ or $(ax+b)^{n}$ → add 1 to the power, divide
$e^{ax+b}$ → divide by $a$
$\dfrac{1}{ax+b}$ → $\dfrac{1}{a}\ln|ax+b|$
$\sin$, $\cos$, $\sec^{2}$ of $ax+b$ → divide by $a$
$\dfrac{f'}{f}$ → $\ln|f|$, $\ f'e^{f}$ → $e^{f}$, $\ f'f^{n}$ → $\dfrac{f^{n+1}}{n+1}$

I DoEpisode 9 · Mixed integrals
Find $\displaystyle\int\left(\dfrac{4x}{x^{2}+5}+6e^{-3x}+\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}\right)dx$.
$\dfrac{4x}{x^{2}+5}=2\times\dfrac{2x}{x^{2}+5}$ is $\dfrac{f'}{f}$: $2\ln(x^{2}+5)$
$6e^{-3x}$ is $e^{ax+b}$: $-2e^{-3x}$
$x^{-2}$ is a power: $-\dfrac{1}{x}$
Total: $2\ln(x^{2}+5)-2e^{-3x}-\dfrac{1}{x}+C$

$\dfrac{(\ln x)^{3}}{3}+C$
We Do 10Episode 9 · Mixed integrals
The gradient of a curve is $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{6x}{x^{2}+1}+e^{-2x}$ and the curve passes through $(0,2)$. Find its equation.
$y=3\ln(x^{2}+1)-\tfrac{1}{2}e^{-2x}+C$
$2=0-\tfrac{1}{2}+C$, so $C=\tfrac{5}{2}$
$y=3\ln(x^{2}+1)-\tfrac{1}{2}e^{-2x}+\tfrac{5}{2}$
