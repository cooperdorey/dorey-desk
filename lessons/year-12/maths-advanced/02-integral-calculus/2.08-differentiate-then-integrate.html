FactEpisode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Integrals reverse derivatives
If $\dfrac{d}{dx}F(x)=g(x)$, then $\displaystyle\int g(x)\,dx=F(x)+C$
A derivative you have already found is a free integral. Integrating a derivative undoes it.
'Hence' means use the result you just found.

Category · ruleEpisode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate-hence-integrate routine
1. Differentiate the given function.
2. Integrate both sides.
3. Move the easy integral across.
4. Divide to get the wanted integral alone.
5. Add $C$ (or use limits).
I DoEpisode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate $x\,e^{2x}$. Hence find $\displaystyle\int x\,e^{2x}\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(xe^{2x}\right)=e^{2x}+2xe^{2x}$
Integrate both sides: $xe^{2x}=\int e^{2x}dx+2\int xe^{2x}dx$
$xe^{2x}=\tfrac{1}{2}e^{2x}+2\int xe^{2x}dx$
$\int xe^{2x}dx=\tfrac{1}{2}\left(xe^{2x}-\tfrac{1}{2}e^{2x}\right)+C$
$=\tfrac{1}{2}xe^{2x}-\tfrac{1}{4}e^{2x}+C$
We Do 1Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate $x\sin x$. Hence find $\displaystyle\int x\cos x\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}(x\sin x)=\sin x+x\cos x$
$x\sin x=-\cos x+\int x\cos x\,dx$
$\int x\cos x\,dx=x\sin x+\cos x+C$
We Do 2Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate $x\ln x$. Hence find $\displaystyle\int\ln x\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}(x\ln x)=\ln x+1$
$x\ln x=\int\ln x\,dx+x$
$\int\ln x\,dx=x\ln x-x+C$
We Do 3Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate $(x^{2}+3)^{5}$. Hence find $\displaystyle\int x(x^{2}+3)^{4}\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^{2}+3)^{5}=5(x^{2}+3)^{4}\times2x=10x(x^{2}+3)^{4}$
$\int x(x^{2}+3)^{4}dx=\dfrac{1}{10}(x^{2}+3)^{5}+C$

We Do 5Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Show that $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\sin x-x\cos x\right)=x\sin x$. Hence evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi}x\sin x\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}(\sin x-x\cos x)=\cos x-\left(\cos x-x\sin x\right)=x\sin x$ ✓
$\int_{0}^{\pi}x\sin x\,dx=[\sin x-x\cos x]_{0}^{\pi}=\left(0+\pi\right)-0=\pi$
Modelled on HSC 2025 Q25.
We Do 6Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate $\dfrac{x}{x^{2}+1}$. Hence evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{1-x^{2}}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}\dfrac{x}{x^{2}+1}=\dfrac{(x^{2}+1)-x\times2x}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}=\dfrac{1-x^{2}}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}$
$\int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{1-x^{2}}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}dx=\left[\dfrac{x}{x^{2}+1}\right]_{0}^{1}=\dfrac{1}{2}-0=\dfrac{1}{2}$
We Do 7Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Show that $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(x^{2}\ln x\right)=2x\ln x+x$. Hence find $\displaystyle\int x\ln x\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}(x^{2}\ln x)=2x\ln x+x^{2}\times\dfrac{1}{x}=2x\ln x+x$ ✓
$x^{2}\ln x=2\int x\ln x\,dx+\dfrac{x^{2}}{2}$
$\int x\ln x\,dx=\dfrac{1}{2}x^{2}\ln x-\dfrac{1}{4}x^{2}+C$
Modelled on HSC 2024 Q27.
We Do 8Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate $x\,e^{-x}$. Hence find the exact value of $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}xe^{-x}\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}(xe^{-x})=e^{-x}-xe^{-x}$
$\int xe^{-x}dx=\int e^{-x}dx-xe^{-x}=-e^{-x}-xe^{-x}$
$[-e^{-x}(1+x)]_{0}^{1}=-2e^{-1}+1=1-\dfrac{2}{e}$
We Do 9Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Differentiate $\dfrac{\ln x}{x}$. Hence find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{\ln x}{x^{2}}\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}\dfrac{\ln x}{x}=\dfrac{1-\ln x}{x^{2}}$
$\dfrac{\ln x}{x}=\int\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}dx-\int\dfrac{\ln x}{x^{2}}dx=-\dfrac{1}{x}-\int\dfrac{\ln x}{x^{2}}dx$
$\int\dfrac{\ln x}{x^{2}}dx=-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}-\dfrac{1}{x}+C$
We Do 10Episode 8 · Differentiate, hence integrate
Show that $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\tan x-x\right)=\tan^{2}x$. Hence evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi/4}\tan^{2}x\,dx$.
$\dfrac{d}{dx}(\tan x-x)=\sec^{2}x-1=\tan^{2}x$ ✓
$[\tan x-x]_{0}^{\pi/4}=\left(1-\tfrac{\pi}{4}\right)-0=1-\tfrac{\pi}{4}$
