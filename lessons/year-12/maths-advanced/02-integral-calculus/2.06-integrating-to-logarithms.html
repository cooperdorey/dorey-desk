Warm-upEpisode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Differentiate each function.
a) $y=\ln x$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{1}{x}$
b) $y=\ln(2x+1)$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{2}{2x+1}$
c) $y=\ln\left(x^{2}+1\right)$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{2x}{x^{2}+1}$
d) Write down a function with derivative $\dfrac{1}{2x+1}$.
Divide (b) by $2$.
$y=\tfrac{1}{2}\ln(2x+1)$ has derivative $\dfrac{1}{2x+1}$.
FactEpisode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Integrating $\frac{1}{x}$
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{x}\,dx=\ln|x|+c,\quad x\neq0$
I DoEpisode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Find $\displaystyle\int\left(x^{2}+\dfrac{5}{x}\right)dx$.
$\int x^{2}\,dx+5\int\dfrac{1}{x}\,dx$
$=\dfrac{x^{3}}{3}+5\ln|x|+c$
We Do 1Episode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Find $\displaystyle\int\left(\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)dx$.
Rewrite: $\int\left(x^{-2}+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)dx$
$\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}$ uses the power rule, and $\dfrac{1}{x}$ gives a logarithm.
$=\dfrac{x^{-1}}{-1}+\ln|x|+c$
$=-\dfrac{1}{x}+\ln|x|+c$
FactEpisode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Integrating $\frac{1}{ax+b}$
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{ax+b}\,dx=\dfrac{1}{a}\ln|ax+b|+c,\quad a\neq0$
I DoEpisode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{4}{3x-1}\,dx$.
$a=3$, so divide by $3$.
$\int\dfrac{4}{3x-1}\,dx=\dfrac{4}{3}\ln|3x-1|+c$
Check: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\ln|3x-1|\right)=\dfrac{4}{3}\times\dfrac{3}{3x-1}=\dfrac{4}{3x-1}$
We Do 2Episode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{3}{2x+1}\,dx$.
$\int_{0}^{1}\dfrac{3}{2x+1}\,dx=\left[\dfrac{3}{2}\ln|2x+1|\right]_{0}^{1}$
$=\dfrac{3}{2}\ln3-\dfrac{3}{2}\ln1$
$=\dfrac{3}{2}\ln3$
FactEpisode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Integrating $\frac{f'(x)}{f(x)}$
$\displaystyle\int\dfrac{f'(x)}{f(x)}\,dx=\ln|f(x)|+c$
I DoEpisode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x}{x^{2}+1}\,dx$.
$f(x)=x^{2}+1$ and $f'(x)=2x$ is on top.
$\int\dfrac{2x}{x^{2}+1}\,dx=\ln\left(x^{2}+1\right)+c$
The modulus is not needed because $x^{2}+1\gt 0$.
We Do 3Episode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^{2}}{x^{3}+5}\,dx$.
$f(x)=x^{3}+5$, so $f'(x)=3x^{2}$.
$=\tfrac{1}{3}\int\dfrac{3x^{2}}{x^{3}+5}\,dx$
$=\tfrac{1}{3}\ln\left|x^{3}+5\right|+c$
We Do 4Episode 6 · Logarithmic functions
Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x}{4+x^{2}}\,dx$.
$f(x)=4+x^{2}$, so $f'(x)=2x$.
$=\tfrac{1}{2}\int\dfrac{2x}{4+x^{2}}\,dx$
$=\tfrac{1}{2}\ln\left(4+x^{2}\right)+c$

We Do 5Episode 6 · Logarithmic functions
The curve $y=\dfrac{3}{x-1}$ intersects the line $y=\dfrac{3}{2}x$ at the point $(2,3)$. The region bounded by the curve, the line, the $x$-axis and the line $x=4$ is shaded in the diagram.
Find the exact area of the shaded region.
Split the region at $x=2$.
Under the line from $x=0$ to $x=2$: $\int_{0}^{2}\tfrac{3}{2}x\,dx=\left[\dfrac{3x^{2}}{4}\right]_{0}^{2}=3$
Under the curve from $x=2$ to $x=4$: $\int_{2}^{4}\dfrac{3}{x-1}\,dx=\left[3\ln|x-1|\right]_{2}^{4}=3\ln3-3\ln1=3\ln3$
Area $=3+3\ln3$ square units.
