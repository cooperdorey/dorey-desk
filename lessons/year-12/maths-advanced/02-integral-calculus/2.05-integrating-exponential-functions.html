Warm-upEpisode 5 · Exponential functions
Differentiate each function.
a) $y=e^{x}$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=e^{x}$
b) $y=e^{3x}$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=3e^{3x}$
c) $y=e^{5x-2}$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=5e^{5x-2}$
d) Write down a function with derivative $e^{3x}$.
Divide (b) by $3$.
$y=\dfrac{1}{3}e^{3x}$ has derivative $e^{3x}$.
FactEpisode 5 · Exponential functions
Integrating $e^{ax+b}$
$\displaystyle\int e^{x}\,dx=e^{x}+C\qquad\int e^{ax+b}\,dx=\dfrac{e^{ax+b}}{a}+C$

The power stays the same. Divide by $a$, the coefficient of $x$.
Check by differentiating your answer.
I DoEpisode 5 · Exponential functions
Find $\displaystyle\int e^{3x-1}\,dx$.
Here $a=3$, so divide by $3$.
$\int e^{3x-1}\,dx=\dfrac{e^{3x-1}}{3}+C$
Check: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\dfrac{e^{3x-1}}{3}=\dfrac{3e^{3x-1}}{3}=e^{3x-1}$
We Do 1Episode 5 · Exponential functions
Find $\displaystyle\int\left(e^{x}+e^{3x}\right)dx$.
$\int e^{x}\,dx=e^{x}$ and $\int e^{3x}\,dx=\dfrac{e^{3x}}{3}$
$=e^{x}+\dfrac{1}{3}e^{3x}+C$
HSC 2020 Q4 (multiple choice).
I DoEpisode 5 · Exponential functions
Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}e^{2x}\,dx$.
$\int_{0}^{1}e^{2x}\,dx=\left[\dfrac{e^{2x}}{2}\right]_{0}^{1}=\dfrac{e^{2}}{2}-\dfrac{e^{0}}{2}=\dfrac{e^{2}-1}{2}$
We Do 2Episode 5 · Exponential functions
Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{2}e^{-x}\,dx$.
Here $a=-1$, so divide by $-1$.
$\int_{0}^{2}e^{-x}\,dx=\left[-e^{-x}\right]_{0}^{2}=-e^{-2}-\left(-e^{0}\right)=1-e^{-2}$
FactEpisode 5 · Exponential functions
Integrating $a^{x}$
$\displaystyle\int a^{x}\,dx=\dfrac{a^{x}}{\ln a}+C,\quad a\gt 0,\ a\neq1$

This comes from $\dfrac{d}{dx}a^{x}=a^{x}\ln a$.
Divide by $\ln a$, not by $a$.
I DoEpisode 5 · Exponential functions
Find $\displaystyle\int2^{x}\,dx$.
$\int2^{x}\,dx=\dfrac{2^{x}}{\ln2}+C$
Check: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\dfrac{2^{x}}{\ln2}=\dfrac{2^{x}\ln2}{\ln2}=2^{x}$
We Do 3Episode 5 · Exponential functions
Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{2}3^{x}\,dx$.
$\int_{0}^{2}3^{x}\,dx=\left[\dfrac{3^{x}}{\ln3}\right]_{0}^{2}=\dfrac{9}{\ln3}-\dfrac{1}{\ln3}=\dfrac{8}{\ln3}$
We Do 4Episode 5 · Exponential functions
Let $P(t)$ be a function such that $\dfrac{dP}{dt}=3000e^{2t}$. When $t=0$, $P=4000$. Find an expression for $P(t)$.
$P(t)=\dfrac{3000e^{2t}}{2}+C=1500e^{2t}+C$
$P(0)=4000$: $1500+C=4000$, so $C=2500$.
$P(t)=1500e^{2t}+2500$

We Do 5Episode 5 · Exponential functions
The region bounded by the graph of $f(x)=8-2^{x}$ and the coordinate axes is shown.
Show that the exact area of the shaded region is $24-\dfrac{7}{\ln2}$.
The curve meets the $x$-axis where $8-2^{x}=0$, so $2^{x}=8$ and $x=3$.
Area $=\int_{0}^{3}\left(8-2^{x}\right)dx$
$=\left[8x-\dfrac{2^{x}}{\ln2}\right]_{0}^{3}$
$=\left(24-\dfrac{8}{\ln2}\right)-\left(0-\dfrac{1}{\ln2}\right)$
$=24-\dfrac{7}{\ln2}$
