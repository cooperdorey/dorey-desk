Find the value of $t$, correct to 2 decimal places, in the formula $t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$ when $h=45$ and $g=9.8$.
Write the formula first.
$t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$
Substitute. $2h$ means $2\times h$.
$t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\times 45}{9.8}}$
The bar groups. Work out the top first.
$t=\sqrt{\dfrac{90}{9.8}}$
Finish under the root. Divide, and keep the full value in the calculator.
$t=\sqrt{9.1836\ldots}$
Now take the root.
$t=3.0304\ldots$
Nothing is left to calculate, so round. The digit after the second decimal place is 0, so round down.
$t=3.03$ (correct to 2 decimal places)
Check: $90\div 9.8=9.183673\ldots$ and $\sqrt{9.183673\ldots}=3.030457\ldots$, so $t=3.03$.

Find the cost of hiring a kayak for 5 hours. Use the formula $C=18+7h$, where $C$ is the cost in dollars and $h$ is the number of hours.

$C=18+7h$

$C=53$
