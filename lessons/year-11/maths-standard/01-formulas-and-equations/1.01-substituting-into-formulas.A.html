I Do 1Episode 1 · Substituting into formulas
Find the value of $t$, correct to 2 decimal places, in the formula $t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$ when $h=45$ and $g=9.8$.
Write the formula first.
$t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2h}{g}}$
Substitute. $2h$ means $2\times h$.
$t=\sqrt{\dfrac{2\times 45}{9.8}}$
The bar groups. Work out the top first.
$t=\sqrt{\dfrac{90}{9.8}}$
Finish under the root. Divide, and keep the full value in the calculator.
$t=\sqrt{9.1836\ldots}$
Now take the root.
$t=3.0304\ldots$
Nothing is left to calculate, so round. The digit after the second decimal place is 0, so round down.
$t=3.03$ (correct to 2 decimal places)
Check: $90\div 9.8=9.183673\ldots$ and $\sqrt{9.183673\ldots}=3.030457\ldots$, so $t=3.03$.
We Do 1Episode 1 · Substituting into formulas
Find the cost of hiring a kayak for 5 hours. Use the formula $C=18+7h$, where $C$ is the cost in dollars and $h$ is the number of hours.
Write the formula first.
$C=18+7h$
Substitute. $7h$ means $7\times h$.
$C=18+7\times 5$
Multiply before adding.
$C=18+35$
$C=53$
The cost is 53 dollars.

Find the value of $V$, correct to 1 decimal place, in the formula $V=2.4+0.37u$ when $u=8.6$.
Write the formula first.
$V=2.4+0.37u$
Substitute. $0.37u$ means $0.37\times u$.
$V=2.4+0.37\times 8.6$
Multiply before adding.
$V=2.4+3.182$
$V=5.582$
Nothing is left to calculate, so round. The digit after the first decimal place is 8, so round up.
$V=5.6$ (correct to 1 decimal place)

If $x=-3.472$, find the value of $x^{2}$, correct to 2 decimal places.
Write the expression first.
$x^{2}$
Substitute. The value is negative, so brackets.
$x^{2}=(-3.472)^{2}$
A negative squared is positive.
$x^{2}=12.0547\ldots$
Nothing is left to calculate, so round. The digit after the second decimal place is 4, so round down.
$x^{2}=12.05$ (correct to 2 decimal places)

Suppose $a=\dfrac{b+5}{c-2}$, where $b=19$ and $c=9$. Find the value of $a$, correct to 3 significant figures.
Write the formula first.
$a=\dfrac{b+5}{c-2}$
Substitute.
$a=\dfrac{19+5}{9-2}$
The bar groups. Work out the top and the bottom first.
$a=\dfrac{24}{7}$
Divide, and keep the full value in the calculator.
$a=3.4285\ldots$
Nothing is left to calculate, so round. The third significant figure is 2 and the digit after it is 8, so round up.
$a=3.43$ (correct to 3 significant figures)

We Do 6Episode 1 · Substituting into formulas
A ball is thrown upwards. Its height $h$ metres above the ground after $t$ seconds is given by $h=1.8+14t-5t^{2}$.
(a) Find the height of the ball when $t=2.35$, correct to 1 decimal place. (1 mark)
Write the formula first.
$h=1.8+14t-5t^{2}$
Substitute. $14t$ means $14\times t$ and $5t^{2}$ means $5\times t^{2}$.
$h=1.8+14\times 2.35-5\times 2.35^{2}$
Square first, then multiply, then add and subtract.
$h=1.8+32.9-27.6125$
$h=7.0875$
Nothing is left to calculate, so round. The digit after the first decimal place is 8, so round up.
$h=7.1$ (correct to 1 decimal place)
The height is 7.1 metres.
(b) A hoop has its rim 3.05 metres above the ground. How far above the rim is the ball at this time? Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place. (2 marks)
From (a), use the unrounded value 7.0875, not 7.1.
Distance above the rim is the height minus 3.05.
Distance $=7.0875-3.05$
$\phantom{\text{Distance}}=4.0375$
Nothing is left to calculate, so round. The digit after the first decimal place is 3, so round down.
Distance $=4.0$ (correct to 1 decimal place)
The ball is 4.0 metres above the rim.
